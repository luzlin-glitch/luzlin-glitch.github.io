Never Back Down

A short autobiography about challenge, growth, and exploration.

About Luz

My name is Luz Lin, and I am currently studying Foreign Languages and Literature at National Tsing Hua University. Growing up in a family that values action, creativity, and continuous learning, I was encouraged to explore new opportunities and challenge myself beyond familiar boundaries. This mindset has shaped the way I approach both learning and life.

One phrase that best describes me is “Never Back Down.” Rather than avoiding uncertainty, I enjoy stepping into unfamiliar situations and discovering what I am capable of achieving. Whether it is leading a student organization, managing international projects, learning new technical skills, or participating in competitions, I see every challenge as an opportunity for growth.

Throughout university, I have explored a wide range of fields, including international volunteer management, digital marketing, photography, video production, sustainability initiatives, small systems development, and data analytics. These experiences have taught me how to communicate with people from different backgrounds, work effectively in teams, and adapt to changing environments.

I believe that meaningful growth happens when people are willing to leave their comfort zones. In the future, I hope to continue combining creativity, communication, technical problem solving, and analytical thinking to create positive impact and connect people across cultures.

Three Keywords

Challenge

I enjoy learning through unfamiliar tasks and new environments.

Connection

I value teamwork, cultural exchange, and communication across backgrounds.

Growth

I believe every project can become a chance to become more capable.
